Tips for Best Results
Homepage Background:
  • Use high-resolution images (at least 1920x1080 pixels)
  • Choose images with good contrast so the white text is readable
  • Landscape/wide photos work best for the welcome section
  • The image will be dimmed automatically to maintain text readability
Facebook Sharing:
  • Use a long-lived Page Access Token (60+ days) to avoid frequent reauthorization
  • Test with auto-sharing disabled first, then enable once confirmed working
  • Post shares include the event title, post title, author username, and post content
  • Concerts, initiatives, archive entries, and competition tables must be published manually
  • Make sure your Facebook Page is published and accessible
