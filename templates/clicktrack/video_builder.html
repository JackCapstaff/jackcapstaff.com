Click track preview

Leave the 0:00 bar blank if it is just a reference. Give each musical marker a bar number and set the meter, tempo shape and cue for the span that follows it. The final hit point only closes the last span: it needs a time and the next bar number. A ramp can use the previous span’s ending tempo; enter a starting BPM if it’s the first span or you want a different starting tempo.
