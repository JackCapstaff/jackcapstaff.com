Adjust timing for a different video speed

Scales all musical hit-point times around video 0:00 and recalculates the section tempi. 101% makes intervals 1% longer; 99% makes them 1% shorter. This does not change YouTube playback speed.

Preview the change before applying it. Applying again scales the current timings again.

Click or tap the rail to seek. Drag a hit point to move it without crossing its neighbours. Use the arrow keys on a hit point or the nudge buttons for 0.1-second adjustments.

Move hit points in time

What should move?

Ripple keeps later hit points spaced apart exactly as they are. Earlier hit points and video 0:00 stay fixed.

To move 0.5 seconds earlier, change 10:00.000 to 9:59.500. Choose the mode above before changing the time.

The selected mode also applies to nudges, playhead moves and timeline dragging.

Performance track preview

Leave the 0:00 bar blank if it is just a reference. Give each musical marker a bar number and set the meter, tempo shape and cue for the span that follows it. Enter skipped score bars on the hit point at the start of that span; they add no time. The final hit point closes the last span. Ramp anchors fix one endpoint or match a neighbouring section; the other tempo is calculated from the hit-point times.
