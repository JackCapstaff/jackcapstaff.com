New click track from video

Choose a local video or load a YouTube link. Use the controls below the video to play, pause, or add a hit point. If focus is inside the YouTube player, use the buttons outside it instead.

Local files are not uploaded and their drafts stay in this tab. Choose Save YouTube draft to save the link and hit points to your account. YouTube needs an internet connection and a video that allows embedding; seeking is not frame-accurate.

Resync hit-point times

Shifts musical hit points together; the 0:00 reference stays fixed. Save the draft and update the track separately.

Hit point timeline

Click or tap the rail to seek. Drag a hit point to move it without crossing its neighbours. Use the arrow keys on a hit point or the nudge buttons for 0.1-second adjustments.

Select a hit point to jump there, then play to review the next one. Use the timeline to find a new time, then choose Move hit point to playhead to replace its time without changing its details.

Hit point details

Tick before editing the number. For example, changing 13 to 12A shifts 14 to 13, 15 to 14, and so on.

Click track preview

Leave the 0:00 bar blank if it is just a reference. Give each musical marker a bar number and set the meter, tempo shape and cue for the span that follows it. Enter skipped score bars on the hit point at the start of that span; they add no time. The final hit point closes the last span. Tempo is calculated from hit-point times; a ramp can use the previous span’s ending tempo.
