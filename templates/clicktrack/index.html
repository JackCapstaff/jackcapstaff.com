Bluetooth / keyboard pedal

For a JOYO JSP-01, select an arrow-key or Page Up / Page Down keyboard mode. Pair it in your device's Bluetooth settings, then click here so this page has keyboard focus. A keyboard pedal does not need the MIDI option.

Choose an action and Single or Double, then select Learn pedal key and press the pedal once to identify its key. In Perform, a double press means two separate presses within 350 ms. A key with a double binding waits briefly before its single action; keys with only a single binding respond immediately.

Forward starts playback, resumes if paused, or queues the next section. Back selects the previous section when stopped or queues it for the next barline while playing. In a silent cue there is no barline, so Back moves immediately unless playback is paused.

    How the fields work
    • Tempo is per beat unit. “Auto” uses the dotted beat in compound time (♩. in 6/8, 9/8, 12/8) and the bottom number otherwise.
    • End tempo (optional) makes a gradual accel./rit. across the whole section.
    • Grouping sets the beats in odd or compound meters, e.g. 3+2 for 5/8 or 2+2+3 for 7/8. Leave it blank for the default.
    • Click: beats only, every bottom-number note (e.g. all quavers), or extra subdivisions. “Silent” shows the beats on screen without any sound.
    • For all six quavers in 6/8, choose Every meter unit. The two dotted-crotchet beats stay accented. Extra subdivisions divide each bottom-number note further.
    • Subdivision sound sets pitch offsets (semitones, ±24) and velocity (0–127, zero mutes) separately for inner meter notes and extra subdivisions. Pitch changes the tone or, for hi-hat, its filter frequency. Main beat accents stay unchanged.
    • Performance notes appear prominently for the current section, including in fullscreen. The preview shows the next section's notes, score bar, meter and tempo. Countdowns include the current bar; repeating sections wait for a cue rather than counting down to an automatic change.
    • Advance: auto moves on after the bar count. Wait for pedal keeps looping (vamp) until you press Next or the pedal. Next/pedal always moves on at the next barline.
    • Pause / cue sections make no sound and wait for the pedal (dialogue, applause, page turns…). The next section starts as soon as you press it.
    • Count-in plays when you start from that section or come out of a pause. It doesn’t play when the music flows straight into it.
    • Score bar (optional) sets the printed bar number at the start of the section. Later sections carry on counting from there.
    • Video timings are calculated from bars, meter and tempo, plus the track offset. Count-ins are preparation, not part of the planned section duration. Timings after cue waits or repeats are estimates (one pass, no assumed wait).
    • Calculate steady tempo accepts seconds, m:ss or h:mm:ss (with decimals), using the section's bars, meter and beat unit. It replaces the tempo and removes any ramp; it does not anchor the section to those video times.
    • Performance clock starts with the music after the initial count-in and includes later cue waits/count-ins. Pause (or Space) freezes audio and time; Resume continues the same beat. Stop (Esc) resets the position. The video clock is a reference, not a connection to a video player.
    • Edit tester floats at the bottom of the screen. Choose a section to loop at its starting tempo, with its meter and click sound. Tap changes that section's saved tempo. The tester ignores count-ins, ramps and section transitions; entering Perform stops it.
